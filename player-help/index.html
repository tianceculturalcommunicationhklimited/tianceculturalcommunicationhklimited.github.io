Lowdeck help

Lowdeck is golf, the card game, on iPhone: you against one to three computer rivals, a grid of 6 or 9 cards in front of each player, and the lowest score after the last hole wins.

Contact

Write to contact@tianceculturalcommunicationhklimited.com. Expect a reply from TIANCE CULTURAL COMMUNICATION (HK) LIMITED within about three business days.

A problem is much easier to reproduce if your message says:

  • which iPhone you use and which iOS version it runs (Settings > General > About);
  • the Lowdeck version, shown in Preferences under About;
  • the table: 6 or 9 cards, how many rivals, which level;
  • what you tapped, what happened and what you expected instead.

Turns and scoring

What is a hole? One deal, played until somebody has every card face up and the others have taken their last turn. A game lasts 3, 9 or 18 holes; choose the length under Holes on the home screen.

How does a turn work? Tap the draw pile or the discard. Then tap the card in your grid that the new card should replace. With Confirm with a second tap switched on (the default), tap that same card once more or press Place here. The replaced card goes face up onto the discard. A card taken from the draw pile can be thrown away instead: press Discard and flip, then turn up one of your face-down cards.

I picked up the discard by mistake. Press Put back, or tap the card in your hand, before you place it. Nothing hidden is revealed by doing so.

Why can't I take a move back? Taking back a draw would show you a card you were never meant to see. Lowdeck guards against slips instead: placing and flipping need a second tap on the same card, two taps in very quick succession count as one, and a move that would end the hole always asks twice. To place with a single tap, turn off Confirm with a second tap in Preferences; the hole-ending move still asks twice.

Suits make no difference.

When does a column score zero? When all of its cards share one rank: a pair in a 6-card grid, three of a kind in a 9-card grid. A pair inside a column of three is counted card by card. A jack and a queen are different ranks, so together they are not a pair, even though both are worth 10.

How many cards do I turn up at the start? Two in a 6-card game, three in a 9-card game.

One of my cards is still face down and I drew a card I don't want. Throw it away and choose Flip it, which ends the hole, or Keep it down, which leaves your last card hidden and lets play continue.

What happens when a hole ends? Each other player gets one final turn, then every card is turned up and each column is scored. Lowdeck plays with this final turn, a common way to play golf; some groups end the hole immediately instead. How it works states the rule the app uses.

Is there a penalty for ending a hole? No. Ending first is no guarantee of the lowest score, though.

Rivals

What do the levels mean? Careful plays from what it can see in its own grid and on the discard. Regular also weighs the odds of the cards nobody has seen yet. Expert also keeps count of every card shown during the hole. All rivals in one game play at the same level.

Can the rivals see my face-down cards? No. They decide using only what everyone at the table can see: the cards turned face up and the discard pile. Face-down cards and the order of the draw pile are out of their reach.

How do I know what a rival did? A line just below the rivals' cards describes each move as it happens. Animation speed in Preferences (Normal, Fast or Instant) sets how quickly they play.

Can I play against other people? Lowdeck is played against computer rivals only.

Saved games and the record

Is my game saved? Yes, after every move. Leave in the table menu returns you to Home with the game kept; Continue picks it up again. End game deletes it.

Where are my results? Tap the bar chart icon on the home screen to open Record: games played and won for each level, and for 6-card and 9-card games the average hole, the best hole and the number of holes played. Reset record in Preferences sets them back to zero.

"The saved game could not be read." The file was damaged. Lowdeck set it aside untouched, and you can start a new game.

"This game was saved by a newer version." Update Lowdeck from the App Store to carry on with that game.

Sound, appearance and language

Sound and vibration. Use Sound and Haptics in Preferences. Sounds respect the Ring/Silent switch and do not interrupt music playing in other apps.

Light or dark. Appearance in Preferences: System, Light or Dark.

Languages. Lowdeck is available in English, French and German and follows the order of languages set on your iPhone. The letters on the cards change with the language: J, Q, K in English; V, D, R in French; B, D, K in German. To run Lowdeck in a different language from the rest of the phone, open Settings, scroll to Lowdeck (newer iOS versions group apps under Apps) and change Language.

Requirements

Any iPhone that runs iOS 17 or a newer version; the game is played in portrait. No internet connection is needed at any point.
